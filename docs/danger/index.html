Demand, not crashesWhere it asks the most

Nobody publishes a crash map for these roads and this project will not invent one. This is a map of how much steering each two kilometres asks for, measured the same way everywhere.

What actually goes wrong

A loaded bike is a different bike

รถที่บรรทุกคือรถคนละคัน

Weight added high and behind the rear axle — a tail pack, a rucksack, a pillion — changes the handling in ways that all show up on a descent.

Brake fade

เบรกเฟด

Dragging a brake down a long descent heats it until it stops converting speed into heat as well as it did. On a small automatic with a drum rear…

Buses and pickups on your line

รถบัสและรถกระบะกินเลน

Route 1095 and the mountain half of Route 108 are two lanes, and the minibuses, songthaews and delivery trucks that serve these towns take a hairpin…

Dogs

สุนัข

Free-roaming dogs are ordinary in Thai villages, and a bike at village speed through a village is what triggers a chase.

Gravel on the apex

กรวดกลางโค้ง

Sand, grit and washed gravel collect on the inside line of a hairpin and at the exit of a cutting. It is the most common surface hazard on this…

Riding after dark

ขี่ตอนกลางคืน

Once the sun is down the circuit has no ambient light at all outside the towns, and a rented bike's headlight is usually a single bulb of unknown age.

Riding in the smoke

ขี่ในควัน

In March and April the measured PM2.5 on this circuit sits above the US 24-hour standard on most days. On a bike you are breathing hard, at volume…

The guesthouse driveway

ทางเข้าที่พักชัน

Hillside guesthouses are reached by short, very steep, often unsealed or wet-concrete ramps. It is where heavy bikes are dropped.

The low sun

แดดต่ำ

Late afternoon on a westward leg in the cool season puts the sun directly down the road. A dusty visor turns it into a white sheet.

Demand is measured the same way everywhere on this network: curves per kilometre in fixed 2 km windows. It says where a road asks the most of a rider. It is NOT a record of where anyone has crashed — no such record is published for these roads, and none is invented here.
