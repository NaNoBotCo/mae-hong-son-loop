Riding after dark

ขี่ตอนกลางคืน

Once the sun is down the circuit has no ambient light at all outside the towns, and a rented bike's headlight is usually a single bulb of unknown age.

Riding in the smoke

The low sun

แดดต่ำ

Late afternoon on a westward leg in the cool season puts the sun directly down the road. A dusty visor turns it into a white sheet.

Demand is measured the same way everywhere on this network: curves per kilometre in fixed 2 km windows. It says where a road asks the most of a rider. It is NOT a record of where anyone has crashed — no such record is published for these roads, and none is invented here.
