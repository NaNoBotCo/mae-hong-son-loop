Chiang Mai

The start and the finish. Both roads out of it — 107 to the north, 108 to the south — are the loop's two ends.

The hinge of the southern leg: east of here Route 108 is four to six lanes to Chiang Mai, west of here it is two lanes for 265 kilometres.
