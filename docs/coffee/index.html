Arabica off the ridge you just rode over

อาราบิก้าจากดอยที่เพิ่งข้ามมา

Northern Thailand grows arabica on the hills this road climbs. The plant wants altitude, shade and a dry harvest — which is exactly what these slopes…

Miang and mountain tea

เมี่ยงและชาบนดอย

Before the coffee, these hills grew tea. Much of it still does, and the older use of the leaf is not a drink at all.

The 7-Eleven

เซเว่น

In the towns, the convenience store is a coffee stop, a water stop, a shade stop and a toilet. It is also the most reliable of the four.

The stall at the top of the climb

เพิงกาแฟบนยอดดอย

Somewhere near the top of most long climbs on this circuit there is a shack selling coffee, with a bench pointed at the valley.
