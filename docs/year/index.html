Doi Mae U-Kho, November

Time it right and you get four things at once

Clean air, the sunflowers out, the waterfalls still running and the lanterns going up.

The full moon of the twelfth lunar month, usually November. The river festival, and the northern lantern festival that shares its dates.

Songkran

สงกรานต์

The hottest days of the year, marked by pouring water. On a motorcycle this is a fact you plan around rather than a festival you attend.

The Akha Swing Festival

เทศกาลโล้ชิงช้าของชาวอาข่า

Yehkuja, the most important festival of the Akha year, held around a tall four-posted village swing. Worth timing a trip for — a different trip.

ทุ่งบัวตอง

Tithonia diversifolia — Mexican sunflower, บัวตอง — flowers across Doi Mae U-Kho in November. It is the one thing on this circuit with a hard date.

ฤดูหนาว

Dry, clear, cold at altitude before dawn, and the busiest and most expensive months on the circuit.
