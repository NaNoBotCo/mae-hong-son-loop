Written 2026-09-19. Nothing below is live. Every option needs your word first.

WHAT IS ALREADY BUILT AND EARNING NOTHING
  The Patreon and Ko-fi line is in the footer of all 192 pages, via the shared roster.
  That is the only money mechanism currently on the site, and it is the fleet default.

THE ONE INSTRUMENT BUILT TO COMPLETION
  /roadbook/ — every leg, every measured number, the fuel gaps, the hazard list and the
  glossary on one page, with print CSS so it comes off a printer folded into a tank bag.
  It is free and it stays free. That is the $0 version and it is the default.

  The paid version, if you want one: the same content as a PDF pack — one card per leg,
  A6, double-sided, plus a one-page emergency card (hospital, police, the Thai for
  'I have fallen', the nearest fuel). It needs no third-party account and nothing
  recurring. It is a file you sell once and never touch again.
  YOUR CALL: price, or no.   Suggested band: 150–300 THB / $5–9.
  Cost to build: about two hours. Cost to run: zero.

THE FORK, WITH NUMBERS

  A. SELL THE PDF ROADBOOK
     Revenue per sale: whatever you set, minus the platform's cut (Ko-fi takes 0% on
     the free tier for one-off shop items; Gumroad takes 10%).
     Worst case: nobody buys it and the free HTML page is still the best thing on the site.
     Needs from you: a price, and a yes.
     Expiry: none. This can sit undone indefinitely without cost.

  B. PAID LISTINGS FOR BUSINESSES ON THE LOOP
     Rental shops in Chiang Mai, guesthouses, coffee places. The machinery is already
     built: data/vocab/sponsors.json is empty and the site renders nothing while it is.
     A filled row prints "Paid listing" to the reader wherever it appears, so it is
     labelled, always.
     Revenue: a flat annual fee per listing. At 1,000 THB/year and 20 listings that is
     20,000 THB/year for work you do once.
     Worst case: you have to chase 20 small businesses for 1,000 baht each, which is a
     job, not an income. And a directory that takes money from the places it lists has
     to be visibly clean about it forever after.
     Needs from you: a price, a yes, and a decision about whether you want that job.
     Expiry: none.

  C. BOOKING AFFILIATES
     Hotel and rental booking commissions. NOT SET UP and I did not set it up — it needs
     accounts in your name, which is yours to open, not mine.
     Revenue: typically 3–6% of a booking.
     Worst case: it puts a commercial incentive on which beds the site recommends, on a
     site whose whole argument is that it shows its sources. I would not do this one.
     Needs from you: accounts, and a yes.

  D. THE DATA
     curves.json, air.json and places.json are already public and CC BY 4.0. The curve
     measurement method is original and nobody else publishes it.
     Revenue: none directly. It is the thing that gets the site cited, which is the
     thing that brings the readers the other three options need.
     No decision needed. It is already done.

MY RECOMMENDATION, WHICH IS NOT A DECISION
  A first, because it is finished-shaped, costs nothing to run, and the free version
  keeps the paid one straight. B only if you actually want to talk to twenty guesthouses.
  C not at all. D is already running.
